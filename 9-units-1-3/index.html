1. much · many · several · a lot of · plenty of · some · any · no

Злічуване (можна порахувати) — many; незлічуване — much; a lot of і plenty of — для обох.

  • Countable (you can count them: shops, friends)
    → many · several · a lot of · plenty of
  • Uncountable (no plural: money, time, milk, homework)
    → much · a lot of · plenty of
  • a lot and plenty need of before a noun
    a lot of people · plenty of bread
  • some → positive sentences, offers and requests
    Would you like some tea? · Can you buy some?
  • any → negatives and questions
    Have you got any pets?
  • no = not … any — the word not moves from the verb to the noun
    I haven’t got any money. → I’ve got no money.
  • never no + not
    I’ve got no money. · Wrong: I haven’t got no money.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. We haven’t got _____ milk. Can you buy some?

milk — незлічуване → much.

2. How _____ apples do you want?

apples — злічувані → many.

3. There’s _____ of water in the bottle – drink some!

water — незлічуване; many і several — тільки зі злічуваними → plenty of.

4. I’ve got _____ friends in my new class – about five.

friends — злічувані, «кілька» → several.

5. We can’t make a cake – we’ve got _____ eggs.

no = not + any разом, тому дієслово без not: we haven’t got any eggs → we’ve got no eggs.

2. Present simple or present continuous?

Звичка або факт — present simple; зараз або план із часом — present continuous (крім like, know, want…).

  • usually · often · every day · on Mondays · a fact → present simple
    she plays
  • now · at the moment · today (happening now) · Look! · Listen! · Quiet! → present continuous
    she is playing
  • a plan + a time (tonight, this Saturday) → present continuous
    I’m visiting my aunt tonight.
  • like · love · want · know · understand · believe · need → present simple, even now
    I know the answer now.
  • continuous = am / is / are + ‑ing
    They are reading.
  • never ‑ing alone as the verb
    she is playing, not she playing

Тепер перевір себе:

1. My mum _____ to work on Mondays.

on Mondays — звичка → present simple; she → drives.

2. Look! It _____ outside.

Look! — відбувається зараз → present continuous.

3. I _____ this exercise now. It’s easy!

understand — дієслово стану, не буває в continuous → present simple навіть зараз.

4. We _____ our grandparents this Saturday. The tickets are ready.

План + час (this Saturday) → present continuous.

5. My sister often _____ pictures in the evening.

often → present simple; she → paints.

3. Present simple or continuous: questions

У питанні do / does або am / is / are стоять перед людиною.

  • present simple: (question word) + do / does + person + verb
    Where does she live?
  • present continuous (now or a plan): (question word) + am / is / are + person + verb‑ing
    Who is she calling?
  • with be — no do
    Where is she?
  • not
    Why doesn’t he walk to school? · Why isn’t she smiling?
  • after does / doesn’t the verb has no ‑s
    Does she like it? · He doesn’t drink coffee.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. how often / your brother / play / football?

Звичка → present simple: How often + does + he + дієслово без -s.

2. who / your sister / visit / this Sunday?

План + час → present continuous: Who + is + she + visiting.

3. why / your friends / not / eat / fish?

Факт, не зараз → present simple: Why + don’t + they + дієслово без -s.

4. what / Jake / wear / now?

now → present continuous: What + is + he + wearing.

4. Word building: un‑ · dis‑ · ‑er · ‑ist · ‑or

un‑ / dis‑ — «не» або протилежне; ‑er / ‑ist / ‑or — людина, яка це робить.

  • un‑ / dis‑ = not, or the opposite
  • adjective → usually un‑
    kind → unkind
  • verb → usually dis‑
    obey → disobey
  • but some words are different — learn with the word
    dishonest · unlock
  • a person: ‑er is the most common
    teach → teacher
  • ‑ist and ‑or — learn with the word
    guitar → guitarist · act → actor · visit → visitor
  • one syllable, ends in one vowel + one consonant (not w, x, y) → double it
    shop → shopper
  • ends in ‑e → drop the e
    bake → baker

Тепер перевір себе:

1. His room is always a mess. He’s very _____ (tidy).

Прикметник → тут un-: untidy.

2. My cousin plays the piano in a band. He’s a _____ (piano).

-ist — вчи зі словом: piano → pianist.

3. Please don’t _____ (connect) the computer – I’m using it!

Дієслово → тут dis-: disconnect.

4. She writes books for children. She’s a _____ (write).

-er — найчастіше; write → writer (e випадає).

5. ‑ing, to or just the verb?

Після одних дієслів — ‑ing, після інших — to, після can / must — просто дієслово.

  • enjoy · don’t mind · can’t stand → ‑ing
    I enjoy baking.
  • want · decide · hope · would like → to + verb
    I’d like to go home.
  • can · must · should · let’s → just the verb (no ‑ing, no to)
    You must stop.
  • like · love · hate → ‑ing or to, both are OK
    I love reading. · I love to read.
  • an action at the start of a sentence, as the subject → ‑ing
    Reading is fun.
  • spelling
    make → making · stop → stopping · play → playing

Тепер перевір себе:

1. My dad enjoys _____ (cook) on Sundays.

enjoy → -ing.

2. My brother hopes _____ (find) a summer job.

hope → to + дієслово.

3. You can _____ (sit) here – this chair is free.

can → просто дієслово, без to і без -ing.

4. _____ (dance) is my favourite hobby.

Дія на початку речення, як підмет → -ing; dance → dancing (e випадає).

6. Adjective + preposition + ‑ing

Після прийменника (at, of, in, on) дієслово завжди з ‑ing.

  • good at · bad at · afraid of · tired of · interested in · keen on
  • after a preposition → ‑ing
    She’s good at skating. · He’s keen on cooking.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. My little sister is afraid _____ the dark.

afraid of.

2. My dad is keen _____ old cars.

keen on.

3. My grandma is good _____ fixing bikes.

good at.

4. My brother is afraid of _____ (fly).

Після прийменника (of) → -ing: afraid of flying.

7. anyone · anything · anywhere · any · any longer

any-слова — у запереченнях і питаннях; у ствердженні — «будь-»: any = будь-який, anything = будь-що.

  • anyone = any person
    Can anyone help me?
  • anything = any thing
    Do you want anything?
  • anywhere = any place
    Are you going anywhere?
  • in negatives and questions
    I can’t see anyone. · Is there anything to eat?
  • in a positive sentence any = it doesn’t matter which
    Take any book. · Ask me anything.
  • any good = good at all (in questions and negatives)
    Is this film any good?
  • not … any longer = not now, but before
    I’m not hungry any longer.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. I looked for my keys, but I couldn’t find them _____.

Місце → anywhere.

2. Is _____ sitting here? Can I take this chair?

Людина → anyone.

3. My grandparents had a dog, but they haven’t got it _____.

not … any longer — раніше був, тепер ні.

4. Sit at _____ table – they’re all free.

У ствердженні any + іменник = будь-який.
