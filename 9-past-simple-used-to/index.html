1. Past simple

Past simple — про одну завершену дію чи подію, про те, що сталося в певний час у минулому, і про те, що регулярно відбувалося в минулому.

When?

We use the past simple to talk about:

  • aa single completed past action or event
    I lost my umbrella.
  • bsomething that happened at a particular time in the past
    We moved to Lviv in 2019.
  • cthings that happened regularly in the past
    Every summer we stayed with my aunt.

How?

  • The past simple is usually formed by adding ‑ed to the infinitive.
    play → played · watch → watched
  • Many common verbs are irregular, and the past simple doesn’t end in ‑ed.
    go → went · buy → bought · swim → swam · write → wrote
  • All irregular verbs: Student’s Book, page 167.
  • We form negatives and questions with did and didn’t (did not) + the infinitive.
    I didn’t call my uncle. · Did you like the cake?
  • The verb to be has two forms in the past: was / wasn’t and were / weren’t.
    I / he / she was tired. · You / we / they were at home.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. We _____ pancakes for breakfast this morning.

Сніданок уже був → past simple від eat — ate.

2. My sister _____ her phone at school yesterday.

leave — неправильне дієслово: leave – left.

3. Sorry, I _____ my essay on Sunday.

Після didn’t — infinitive: didn’t finish.

4. _____ you see the match on TV yesterday?

Є дієслово see → Did + you + see.

5. Where _____ you buy that jacket?

Wh‑питання: Where + did + you + buy.

6. Tom _____ at school on Monday – he was ill.

Іншого дієслова немає → was / wasn’t; Tom — один → wasn’t.

2. used to

used to — про те, що регулярно відбувалося (або не відбувалося) в минулому, і про постійну ситуацію в минулому, якої тепер уже немає.

When?

We use used to to talk about:

  • asomething that happened or didn’t happen regularly in the past
    I used to play the piano every day.
  • ba permanent situation in the past that is no longer the case
    We used to have a garden. (We don’t have it now.)
  • We use used to + verb to talk about habits or situations in the past which are not the same now.
    I used to read comics. (Now I read books.)

How?

  • Positive
    I, He, She, We, You, They used to go
    She used to work in a café.
  • Negative
    I, He, She, We, You, They didn’t use to go
    My dad didn’t use to cook.
  • Questions
    Did I, he, she, we, you, they use to go?
    Did you use to like maths?

Тепер перевір себе:

1. At primary school, I _____ the same song every day.

Ствердження: used to + verb (infinitive): used to sing.

2. My grandad _____ a car – he always walked.

Заперечення: didn’t use to — після didn’t use без ‑d.

3. _____ you use to collect stickers?

Питання: Did + you + use to + collect.

4. Where _____ your parents use to live?

Wh‑питання: Where + did + your parents + use to + live.

5. My brother _____ coffee, but now he drinks it every morning.

Зараз п’є, а раніше ні → didn’t use to drink.

6. We _____ a dog, but now we have two cats.

Раніше був пес, тепер його немає → used to have.

3. Past simple or used to?

Один раз або в певний час → past simple; регулярно в минулому → past simple або used to; постійна ситуація, якої вже немає → used to.

When?

  • past simplea single completed past action or event
    I dropped my keys yesterday.
  • past simplesomething that happened at a particular time in the past
    Jake visited Paris in 2022.
  • used toa permanent situation in the past that is no longer the case
    I used to be afraid of dogs.
  • past simplethings that happened regularly in the past
    As a child, I played chess every weekend.
  • used tosomething that happened or didn’t happen regularly in the past
    As a child, I used to play chess every weekend.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. On my last birthday we _____ to a concert.

Останній день народження — один раз → past simple: went.

2. I _____ meat, but now I’m a vegetarian.

Раніше я їв м’ясо, тепер ні → used to eat.

3. Jake _____ a prize in 2023.

in 2023 — одна подія → past simple: won.

4. My dad _____ a beard, but now he shaves every morning.

Раніше була борода, тепер її немає → used to have.

5. I _____ my grandma yesterday.

yesterday — один раз → past simple: visited.

6. My uncle _____ a taxi driver, but now he works in a bank.

Ситуація в минулому, якої вже немає → used to be.
