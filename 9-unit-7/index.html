1. Second Conditional

Second Conditional — imaginary situations (now or in the future)

If + past simple, would / ’d + verb

Говоримо про те, чого насправді немає або що навряд чи станеться: «якби…, то…».

Examples

  • If I had a dog, I’d walk it every morning.
    (у мене немає собаки)
  • We’d go to the sea if Dad didn’t work in July.
    (частини можна поміняти; кома — тільки коли if спершу)
  • If I were taller, I’d play basketball.
    were — для всіх: I / he / she / it were
  • If I were you, I’d tell the truth.
    порада: «на твоєму місці…»
  • She wouldn’t be so tired if she went to bed earlier.
    заперечення: wouldn’t
  • Would you live in another country if you had the chance?
    питання: Would + you + verb

Типові помилки

  • ✗ If I would have a dog, …
    ✓ If I had a dog, …
    у Second Conditional після if не ставимо would
  • ✗ Where you would go …?
    ✓ Where would you go …?
    would перед you
  • ✗ If I had time, I will call you.
    ✓ If I had time, I would call you.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. If I _____ a bigger bedroom, I’d put a desk by the window.

Після if — past simple: If I had. У Second Conditional після if не ставимо would.

2. If my brother lived closer, we _____ football together every weekend.

Друга частина — would + дієслово.

3. If Oksana _____ taller, she would join the volleyball team.

Уявна ситуація: were для всіх — I / he / she / it were.

4. If my parents _____ so strict, I’d stay out later.

Після if — past simple, заперечення: weren’t.

5. If our school had a pool, I _____ swim every day.

had після if → у другій частині would (’d).

2. First or Second? · Real → imaginary

First or Second?

  • First
    If + present simple, will + verb
    If it rains tomorrow, we’ll stay at home.
    реально, може статися
  • Second
    If + past simple, would + verb
    If it rained every day, we’d stay at home all the time.
    уявно, так не є

Уявляємо протилежне до того, що є насправді (I don’t have a car… / We are busy…) → Second.

Real → imaginary (a task from the test)

  • Реальність навпаки: так → не, не → так.
  • if-частина → past (had / didn’t / weren’t / could)
  • інша частина → would / wouldn’t + verb (can’t → would be able to або could).
  • Пропуск може бути в будь-якій частині.
  • Скорочення (I’d, didn’t) — одне слово.
  • Max is busy, so he can’t help us.
    → If Max weren’t busy, he would be able to help us.
  • Lena is shy. She doesn’t sing in the school choir.
    → She’d sing in the school choir if she weren’t shy.
  • My sister has a cold. She won’t come to the party.
    → If my sister didn’t have a cold, she’d come to the party.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. If it _____ sunny tomorrow, we’ll have a picnic.

Реально може статися (tomorrow), а далі will → First: if + present simple.

2. I don’t have a bike. If I _____ a bike, I’d ride it to school.

Велосипеда насправді немає → уявляємо → Second: If I had.

3. Taras is ill, so he can’t play football today. → _____

Реальність навпаки: ill → weren’t ill; can’t → would be able to (або could).

4. We live in a small flat. We can’t get a dog. → _____

Так → не: live → didn’t live; can’t → would be able to (або could).

3. who · which · that · where · when · whose

Relative clauses — who · which · that · where · when · whose

  • who людина
    the girl who sings in the choir
  • which річ, тварина
    the bike which I got for my birthday
  • that людина або річ — ТІЛЬКИ без ком
    the film that we watched yesterday
  • where місце
    the village where my grandma grew up
  • when час
    the day when we moved to Kyiv
  • whose чий · ’s / his / her / its / their → whose
    a boy whose cousin is a famous singer

Ця частина стоїть одразу після слова, яке пояснює: the girl who …, my uncle, who …

Тепер перевір себе:

1. That’s the teacher _____ helped me with my project.

Людина → who.

2. I lost the phone _____ my parents gave me.

Річ → which.

3. This is the café _____ we have lunch on Saturdays.

Місце, і речення повне (we have lunch there) → where.

4. Do you remember the summer _____ we went to the Carpathians?

Час → when.

5. Do you know the girl _____ mum works at the pharmacy?

Чия мама? → whose (замість his / her).

6. My grandad, _____ is eighty, still goes fishing.

Після коми that не можна; людина → who.

4. Defining vs non-defining · Two sentences → one

Defining vs non-defining clauses

Прибери цю частину. Зміст змінився?

  • Students who finish early can go home.
    → Students can go home.
    Усі? — зміст змінився → defining · без ком · that можна
  • I saw Ed Davies, who lives in my street.
    → I saw Ed Davies.
    те саме → non-defining · з комами · that НЕ можна

Can we drop it?

who / which / that — так, коли далі підмет: the film (that) we watched. Ні — коли далі дієслово або є коми. Не впевнений — лиши.

which or where?

  • My phone, which is very old, still works well.
    одразу дієслово (is) → which
  • the bike which I got for my birthday
    у реченні бракує слова: I got ___ (it) → which
  • Kyiv is the city where I was born.
    речення повне: I was born (there) → where

Two sentences → one (a task from the test)

  • I have a cousin. She plays the drums.
    → I have a cousin who plays the drums.
  • That’s the bakery. We buy bread there.
    → That’s the bakery where we buy bread.
  • My aunt is a nurse. She works at night.
    → My aunt, who is a nurse, works at night.
  • I know a girl. Her dad is a pilot.
    → I know a girl whose dad is a pilot.
  • The boy plays the violin. His mum is a famous singer.
    → The boy whose mum is a famous singer plays the violin.

Типові помилки

  • ✗ My aunt, that is a nurse, …
    ✓ My aunt, who is a nurse, …
    після коми не that
  • ✗ the bakery where we buy bread there
    ✓ the bakery where we buy bread
    there / here уже в where

Тепер перевір себе:

1. Which sentence is correct?

З комами (non-defining) — тільки who / which, не that.

2. The boys who play in our team train every day. Remove who play in our team. Does the meaning change?

Без цієї частини — «усі хлопці тренуються», зміст змінився → defining, без ком.

3. We have a neighbour. He fixes old cars. → _____

Людина → who, і who замінює he — удруге he не пишемо.

4. That’s the shop. I buy my comics there. → _____

there уже всередині where.

5. I met a woman. Her son plays for Dynamo. → _____

Her son → whose son. who’s = who is / who has.

6. The bus _____ goes to my school is always late.

Одразу дієслово (goes) і це річ → which.
