8.2 The passive

Wider World 3, урок 8.2 — Present і Past Simple passive.

Як працювати
  1. Прочитай правило.
  2. Одразу під ним — завдання. Обери a, b або c.
  3. Одна спроба. Після відповіді побачиш правильний варіант і чому.
  4. Є помилки? Перечитай правило і натисни «Ще раз цей блок».

1. Present Simple passive

Пасив — коли важливо, що сталося з річчю, а не хто це зробив. Підмет не робить дію — дія відбувається з ним.

  • Active: the subject does it
    People grow rice in China.
  • Passive: something happens to the subject
    Rice is grown in China.
  • Present Simple passive = am / is / are + past participle
    The classroom is cleaned every day.
  • one thing → is
    Milk is sold here.
  • many things → are
    Eggs are sold here.
  • not → isn’t / aren’t + past participle
    Dogs aren’t allowed in the park.
  • question → Is / Are + subject + past participle
    Is English spoken here?
  • a lot of + uncountable (water, money) → is
    A lot of water is used here.
  • a lot of + plural (cars, phones) → are
    A lot of cars are sold here.
  • people = plural → are
    People are told to wait.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. Football _____ all over the world.

Football — одне → is + played.

2. These T‑shirts _____ in Bangladesh.

T‑shirts — багато → are + made.

3. The library _____ by Mrs Bondar at eight every morning.

Є by Mrs Bondar — бібліотеку відчиняє людина → is opened.

4. Phones _____ in our school canteen.

Телефонам не дозволяють → заперечення в пасиві: aren’t + allowed.

5. _____ breakfast served at seven in this hotel?

Питання в пасиві: Is + breakfast + served.

6. A lot of coffee _____ in Italy.

coffee — незлічуване, одне → is, хоч і a lot of.

7. In hospitals, people _____ to wash their hands.

people — багато (множина) → are told.

2. The third form (past participle)

У пасиві — завжди третя форма. Правильні дієслова: + ‑ed. Неправильні — вчимо (SB, стор. 136).

  • regular verbs → + ‑ed
    clean → cleaned · use → used · stop → stopped
  • irregular verbs → learn them
    make → made · build → built · find → found
  • sometimes the second and third forms are different
    write – wrote – written
  • more
    take → taken · give → given · see → seen · eat → eaten
  • more
    know → known · speak → spoken · steal → stolen · hide → hidden
  • more
    send → sent · catch → caught · tell → told · sell → sold
  • in the passive — always the third form
    is taken, not is took

Тепер перевір себе:

1. This song was _____ by my brother.

write – wrote – written: у пасиві — третя форма.

2. Tea is _____ in many countries.

drink – drank – drunk: після is — третя форма.

3. The pizza was _____ in five minutes.

eat – ate – eaten.

4. My keys were _____ under the sofa.

find – found – found: неправильне дієслово.

5. The last piece of cake was _____ by my sister.

take – took – taken.

3. Past Simple passive

Те саме в минулому: was / were + третя форма. Одне — was, багато — were.

  • Past Simple passive = was / were + past participle
    The bridge was built in 1995.
  • one thing → was
    The cup was broken.
  • many things → were
    The cups were broken.
  • not → wasn’t / weren’t
    The tickets weren’t sold online.
  • question → Was / Were + subject + past participle
    Were you invited?
  • active Did …? → passive Was / Were …?
    Did they open the shop? → Was the shop opened?
  • active didn’t → passive wasn’t / weren’t
    They didn’t paint the door. → The door wasn’t painted.
  • yesterday · last year · in 2010 · ago → was / were

Тепер перевір себе:

1. Our school _____ in 1968.

1968 — минуле, школу збудували люди → was built.

2. Two new computers _____ to our class last week.

last week — минуле; computers — багато → were given.

3. I _____ to Oksana’s party, so I didn’t go.

Мене не запросили → пасив; I → wasn’t invited.

4. _____ your bike repaired last month?

Питання в пасиві минулого: Was + your bike + repaired.

5. Yesterday’s match _____ because of the rain.

Матч не скасовує себе сам; yesterday → was cancelled.

4. by + who did it

Хто це зробив — через by. Невідомо хто або неважливо — by не пишемо.

  • who did it → by + person
    This cake was baked by my grandma.
  • we don’t know who → no by
    My phone was found in the park.
  • it isn’t important who → no by
    Bread is sold here.
  • by people · by someone · by them say nothing → we drop them
    Rice is grown in China. (not: is grown by people)
  • but if the words add information, keep them
    This song is loved by millions of fans.

Тепер перевір себе:

1. My grandad made this table. → This table _____ .

Хто зробив — через by: was made by my grandad.

2. Which sentence is better?

Хто миє — неважливо, тож by не пишемо.

3. Lesia Ukrainka wrote this poem. → This poem _____ Lesia Ukrainka.

wrote — минуле → was written by.

4. They sell fresh bread at this bakery. → Fresh bread _____ at this bakery.

Хліб продають; They — «люди взагалі», тож by не потрібне; bread — одне → is sold.

5. Our coach chose the team. → The team _____ .

choose – chose – chosen; минуле → was chosen by our coach.

6. Did they fix the lift? → _____ the lift fixed?

Did в активі → Was у пасиві: Was the lift fixed?

7. They didn’t finish the new bridge. → The new bridge _____ .

didn’t в активі → wasn’t + третя форма в пасиві.

5. Active or passive? Now or past?

Спершу: підмет робить дію сам (active) чи з ним щось роблять (passive)? Потім: зараз чи в минулому?

  • the subject does it → active
    My sister feeds the cat.
  • something happens to the subject → passive
    The cat is fed twice a day.
  • usually · every day · often (now) → am / is / are
  • yesterday · last week · in 2020 · ago → was / were

Тепер перевір себе:

1. My dad _____ the dishes every evening.

Тато сам миє посуд → active: washes.

2. The dishes _____ an hour ago, so we can have tea now.

Посуд помили (він не миє сам); an hour ago → were washed.

3. These days, this app _____ by lots of students.

Застосунком користуються; these days — зараз → is used.

4. The first football World Cup _____ in Uruguay in 1930.

Чемпіонат провели; 1930 → was played.

5. Today Ukrainian _____ in Ukraine and in many other countries.

Мовою розмовляють; today — зараз → is spoken.
